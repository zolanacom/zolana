What stays private?

Native shielded sends hide the payment amount and both private addresses from the public ledger. Your wallet creates proofs and decrypts received payments on this device.

Native private sends pay fees from your private balance, without a public gas balance of your own. The shared relay account, fee, timing and nullifiers remain public. Public deposits and withdrawals reveal amounts and public accounts; withdrawals still need a funded fee account.

The invited gateway knows your membership and receiving address. It sees your IP address and request timing, and can link your submissions. Your spending and viewing keys stay on this device.

This experimental Zolana network uses test coins only. It has no Solana bridge or post-quantum protection.
