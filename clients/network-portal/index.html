Your privacy
has a boundary.

Zolana is an independent blockchain for native shielded deposits, private transfers and withdrawals. The browser wallet creates proofs and decrypts its own notes locally.

Shielded details

Payment amounts and receiving addresses are protected by proofs. Your spending and viewing keys stay on your device.

Public evidence

Blocks, timing, fees, nullifiers and public deposit or withdrawal accounts and amounts remain visible.

The invited gateway knows your membership and receiving address. It sees your IP address and request timing and can link your submissions. This is not a claim of complete anonymity.

Valueless test coins. No Solana bridge, SOL/USDC deposits, post-quantum protection or audited-security claim.
