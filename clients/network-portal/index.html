Privacy is part
of the protocol.

Zolana is an independent blockchain. Its validators execute native shielded deposits, private transfers and withdrawals. The wallet is your interface to that network.

Private payment details

Shielded amounts and recipients are protected by proofs. Your wallet decrypts its own notes.

Public network evidence

Blocks, fee payers, fees, timing, nullifiers and public deposit or withdrawal amounts remain visible.

Local test coins only. No Solana bridge, mainnet settlement or audited-security claim.
