# Post-quantum migration roadmap

Status: planned research and protocol migration, not implemented. Zolana currently uses Ed25519 public/fee and validator authorities and Orchard 0.16/Halo2 shielded payments. Neither the current chain nor its private payments are claimed to resist a cryptographically relevant quantum computer. No activation date or algorithm choice is committed. This work does not silently alter existing genesis, wallets, notes, backups or nullifiers and is not a prerequisite for the limited experimental invite-only testnet.

| Stage | Deliverable | Acceptance gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Inventory and threat model | Inventory wallet/fee signatures, validator votes/identity/network authentication, Orchard authorization/proofs/commitments/nullifiers/note encryption, backup derivation/encryption and release/transport trust. Separate theft/inflation, future ciphertext exposure, consensus and recovery threats. | Specialist review of assumptions and security scope; versioned migration proposal with transaction size, proof time, verification cost, storage and denial-of-service budgets. No new security claim. |
| 2. Hybrid wallet authorization prototype | Evaluate reviewed implementations of standardized PQ signatures, starting with ML-DSA as a candidate. Require both classical and PQ authorization over the same network-, operation-, expiry- and replay-bound intent; no fallback to either signature. Design native enforcement rather than a gateway-only check. | Consensus/runtime rejection of missing, invalid, downgraded or cross-network signatures; bounded cost measurements, key rotation, seed/backup/restore compatibility and crash/restart tests. A public PQ co-signer must not link shielded activity unintentionally. Any claim is limited to this authorization boundary. |
| 3. Validator migration | Design versioned hybrid identity/vote and relevant network authentication, activation rules and operator key custody/rotation. Audit upstream dependencies and signed consensus artifacts, not just wallet transactions. | Mixed-version and adversarial partition/rejoin tests, tower/snapshot/restart compatibility, replay/downgrade rejection and independent consensus review before activation. Wallet PQ authorization alone does not satisfy this gate. |
| 4. Shielded protocol and fund migration | Research a reviewed quantum-resistant proof/commitment/authorization and note-encryption design, or a separately scoped recoverability transition. Specify supply conservation, old/new pool accounting, nullifier continuity, address/key/backup versions and migration of existing notes before any old scheme is retired. | Independent cryptographic analysis and implementation review, malicious-proof/inflation/double-spend tests, old-wallet migration and restore/restart rehearsals with exact supply reconciliation. Existing Orchard notes do not automatically acquire recoverability or confidentiality from a new signature scheme. |
| 5. Reviewed activation | Publish the exact protection scope, unresolved dependencies, activation/version rules, measured costs and user migration/recovery procedure. Treat retirement of an unsafe old scheme and rollback safety explicitly. | Independent security review, rehearsed network upgrade and funded-note migration; broaden claims only to the boundaries actually implemented and reviewed. No generic “quantum-proof” promise. |

Hybrid authorization is a possible first experiment, not full-chain PQ security. Protecting future signatures also does not retroactively protect previously published ciphertexts. Quantum recoverability, PQ spend authorization and PQ confidentiality are distinct properties; each needs its own design and evidence.

Primary references checked for this plan: [NIST FIPS 204, ML-DSA](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/204/final), including its current errata; and the **proposed**, not automatically adopted, [ZIP 2005 quantum-recoverability design](https://zips.z.cash/zip-2005). ZIP 2005 identifies Orchard commitment binding as a separate problem from replacing its proof system. It is not a drop-in upgrade to this fork. The [Squads demonstration](https://x.com/gansersol/status/2110056332023714037) is scoped multisig authorization; it is not evidence of chain-wide PQ consensus or shielded security.
